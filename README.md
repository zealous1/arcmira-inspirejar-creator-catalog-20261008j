## Analytics & Channel Insights

- **[Social Blade](https://socialblade.com)** — Tracks public YouTube channel statistics and historical growth trends.
- **[TubeBuddy](https://www.tubebuddy.com)** — Browser extension for channel management, A/B thumbnail testing, and analytics.
- **[vidIQ](https://vidiq.com)** — Dashboard and browser extension providing keyword scoring, channel audits, and competitor tracking.
- **[Creator Studio Analytics](https://studio.youtube.com)** — YouTube's own built-in analytics dashboard for watch time, audience retention, and traffic sources.
- **[Morningfame](https://morningfa.me)** — YouTube analytics tool focused on SEO scoring and growth recommendations for smaller channels.

## SEO & Keyword Research

- **[TubeBuddy Keyword Explorer](https://www.tubebuddy.com)** — YouTube-specific keyword search volume and competition scoring.
- **[vidIQ Keyword Tool](https://vidiq.com)** — Suggests tags and keywords scored for YouTube search and suggested-video ranking.
- **[Google Trends](https://trends.google.com)** — Free tool showing search interest over time, filterable to YouTube search.
- **[Keywordtool.io](https://keywordtool.io)** — Generates keyword suggestions pulled from YouTube's autocomplete data.
- **[AnswerThePublic](https://answerthepublic.com)** — Visualizes common questions and phrases people search around a topic.

## Video Editing & Thumbnails

- **[DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve)** — Free, professional-grade video editing and color-grading software.
- **[Adobe Premiere Pro](https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere.html)** — Subscription-based, industry-standard video editing software.
- **[CapCut](https://www.capcut.com)** — Free cross-platform editor, widely used for short-form video.
- **[Canva](https://www.canva.com)** — Design tool with templates for YouTube thumbnails and channel art.
- **[Photopea](https://www.photopea.com)** — Free browser-based image editor with a Photoshop-like interface, usable for thumbnail design.
- **[Shotcut](https://shotcut.org)** — Free, open-source cross-platform video editor.

## Promotion & Growth Services

- **[TubeBuddy Promote](https://www.tubebuddy.com)** — In-platform tools for scheduling and cross-promoting video releases.
- **[Promolta](https://www.promolta.com)** — Service offering YouTube video promotion aimed at increasing views and subscribers.
- **[Veefly](https://veefly.com)** — Platform offering YouTube promotion and view/subscriber growth services.
- **[Prodvigate](https://prodvigate.com)** — YouTube promotion service targeting views, subscribers, and engagement.
- **[ArtistPush](https://artistpush.me)** — Promotion service covering YouTube alongside Spotify and Instagram growth.
- **[Fiverr (YouTube Promotion category)](https://www.fiverr.com)** — Freelancer marketplace with a dedicated category for YouTube promotion gigs.

## Content Planning & Scheduling

- **[Airtable](https://airtable.com)** — Spreadsheet-database hybrid for tracking video ideas and publishing schedules.
- **[Arcmira: YouTube Transcript Search](https://arcmira.com/)** — Finds timestamped passages in indexed YouTube videos and livestreams for source research and clip discovery, with [API access](https://arcmira.com/docs).
- **[Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com)** — Widely used for scheduling recording, editing, and publishing deadlines.
- **[Notion](https://www.notion.so)** — Flexible workspace commonly used for content calendars and script drafting.
- **[Trello](https://trello.com)** — Kanban-style board tool for planning video production pipelines.
- **[TubeBuddy Schedule](https://www.tubebuddy.com)** — Bulk scheduling and publish-time optimization for YouTube uploads.

## Community & Engagement Tools

- **[Discord](https://discord.com)** — Platform for building and moderating a channel community via text and voice.
- **[Patreon](https://www.patreon.com)** — Membership platform for offering paid tiers and exclusive content to subscribers.
- **[YouTube Community Tab](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7574549)** — Native YouTube feature for posting updates, polls, and images between videos.
- **[StreamElements](https://streamelements.com)** — Chatbot and engagement tools for channels that livestream.
- **[Circle](https://circle.so)** — Community platform for hosting discussions and events outside of YouTube itself.

## Learning Resources

- **[YouTube Creator Academy](https://creatoracademy.youtube.com)** — Official YouTube courses on channel growth, monetization, and production.
- **[YouTube Help Center](https://support.google.com/youtube)** — Official documentation on platform policies, monetization rules, and features.
- **[r/NewTubers](https://www.reddit.com/r/NewTubers/)** — Community subreddit where creators share advice and channel feedback.
- **[r/PartneredYoutube](https://www.reddit.com/r/PartneredYoutube/)** — Subreddit for monetized creators discussing growth and platform changes.
- **[Think Media (YouTube channel)](https://www.youtube.com/@ThinkMediaTV)** — Long-running channel covering video gear, strategy, and the creator business.
